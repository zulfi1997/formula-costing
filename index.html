Compounding Formula Costing

Load the Item Valuation Summary, then a master formula record (Excel, Word or PDF). Every formula in the file is matched to inventory items and costed per unit. Review the matches, add provisions, and copy the result into Excel.

Files are read in your browser only. Nothing is uploaded.

Step 4

Provisions & pricing

An estimate until the real volume is recorded. It is costed from the working dilution (e.g. 1 M NaOH / HCl) and its raw materials are added to the production plan. Set 0 to leave pH adjustment out.

Provisions apply to every formula, in the order listed. “% of running total” includes everything above it. Saved in this browser.

Step 5

Step 6

Formula review: gaps & recommendations

Checks every formula against the inventory file and its own data: missing items, costs, units, volumes, packaging, stock and the warning notes written in the formula record. These are data checks. They do not replace a pharmacist's review of a formulation.
